Planning a refinish

What changes the look and the price of a refinish

Two floors of the same size can be very different jobs. The wood species, the color you want, and the condition of the boards all shape the work.

How the woods found in Rhode Island homes take a finish
WoodWhat to expect
Red oakSands and stains evenly. Its pink cast shows under natural, gray, and very light colors.
White oakSands and stains evenly. A good base for natural, light, and gray tones.
Maple and birchDense, tight-grained woods that can take stain unevenly. They are often kept natural, or need extra care with color.
Pine and firSofter woods found in some of the oldest houses. They mark more easily and can take stain unevenly, so test a color before committing.

Natural or stained

A clear finish shows the wood's own color. Stain adds a step and makes color the main decision. Look at samples on your own floor once it is sanded, since the same stain looks different on each species and in each room's light.

Oil-based or water-based finish

Oil-based polyurethane adds a warm amber tone that deepens over time. It dries slowly and has a stronger smell. Water-based finish stays clearer, dries faster, and has less odor. Ask which one your quote is based on and how many coats it includes.

What changes the price

Square footage is the starting point. Stairs, board repairs, a stain color, deep wear or pet stains, and carpet or adhesive that has to come up first all add to the scope. Sanding and refinishing usually costs less than replacing the floor.

Living through the work

Rooms need to be empty while the floor is sanded and finished. When you can walk on it, move furniture back, and lay rugs depends on the finish. Get those three times in writing for the product being used.

Floor refinishing questions

The first decision is whether the existing hardwood has enough sound material to save.

Can old hardwood floors be refinished instead of replaced?

Often, yes. If the wood is structurally sound and has enough material left, sanding and refinishing can restore the existing floor without replacing it.

Can damaged boards be repaired during refinishing?

Individual damaged boards can often be repaired or replaced before sanding when the rest of the hardwood floor is worth saving.

How much does hardwood floor refinishing cost in Rhode Island?

The price depends on the square footage, the number of stairs, board repairs, whether you want a stain color, and the finish selected. Sanding and refinishing usually costs less than replacing the floor. We inspect the existing hardwood and give you a clear written quote before work starts.

Can I change the color of my hardwood floors?

Yes. Sanding takes the floor back to bare wood, so it can be finished natural or stained a new color. The species limits the result. Red oak carries a pink cast under light colors, and maple can take stain unevenly.

How long do I have to stay off refinished floors?

It depends on the finish, the number of coats, and the conditions in the house. Ask for the walk-on time, the furniture time, and the rug time for the specific product. Do not assume every floor can be walked on the same day.
